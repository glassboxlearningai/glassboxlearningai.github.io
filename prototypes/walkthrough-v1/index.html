Before we meet Maya

Two tutors

Same student. Same question. Two different AI tutors.

Student · sent to both tutors

i have to invent something inspired by nature for science. i found a leaf where the water rolls right off. what should i invent?

ATutor A

Great find! That's called the lotus effect. The leaf is covered in tiny waxy bumps that trap air, so water beads up and rolls off, taking dirt with it.

Here are three inventions you could make:

  1. A self-cleaning window coating
  2. A raincoat that never gets soaked
  3. Paint that keeps buildings clean

The raincoat is the easiest to explain. Want me to write up your design description too?

YES omg that would save me. thank you!!

Here you go! “My invention is a raincoat inspired by the lotus leaf. Its surface copies the leaf's tiny waxy bumps, so rain…”

BTutor B

Cool find! What did you notice when you touched the leaf?

it felt waxy. and it was dry under the drops

So the leaf keeps water out. Where in your life do you wish water would roll off like that?

my backpack. my homework got soaked last week

That's a real problem to solve. What would your backpack need to copy from the leaf?

Robot

Hi Maya! I'm a robot, a computer program. Here's what I can and can't do with your photo.

I can seeA green leaf with round, shiny drops of water on it.
I can'tFeel it, smell it, or watch the drops roll. You did that.

So you're the expert on this leaf. On your sketch you wrote “DRY under here!” What did that feel like?

Robot dashboardOffOn

How sure am I?

These are drops of water on a leaf Very sure
What kind of plant this is Not sure. Maybe an elephant ear?

What I'm using

  • Your photo (only the pixels)
  • The words on your sketch
  • Patterns from lots of photos I learned from
  • How the leaf feels. Only you know that.
Put on X-ray glassesSee how I got my answer
X-ray glassesHow I got my answer

Your photo is 640 × 480 pixels. That's 307,200 tiny colored squares.

The circled square is red · green · blue.

  1. I turned your photo into numbers

    Every square is just three numbers: how red, how green, how blue.

  2. I matched patterns

    Round, bright spots with dark edges look like lots of photos of water drops I learned from. So I'm very sure about the drops.

  3. I guessed the plant

    The leaf's shape is a bit like a few plants I know. None of them match well, so I'm not sure.

  4. I hit my limits

    Nothing in the pixels tells me if the leaf is waxy, or dry under the drops. That's why I asked you.

Our goals

Building a better way to use AI in middle school

Some AI use in K–12 is inevitable. We're building a model for how middle schools can use it well. That model has three goals.

  1. Goal 1

    Builds thinking, doesn't replace it

    The AI doesn't do students' thinking for them. It helps them think further than they would on their own.

  2. Goal 2

    Shows how AI works

    A glass box, not a black box. Students learn how AI works while they use it.

  3. Goal 3

    Balances screens with real experiences

    Going outside, hands-on projects, journals and working with classmates take up most of the time. The AI is one small part.

Why we asked you here

Your feedback shapes what we build

It shapes our design

Everything you'll see today is an early idea. What you tell us changes what we build next.

NSF wants to hear from you

The National Science Foundation funds this project and wants to see what parents and teachers think. We'll include what we learn in our reports, with names removed.

Honest and critical is more useful to us than polite.

The leaf that cleans itself

Lotus · plant · a nature story
The story

Lotus plants grow up out of muddy ponds, but their leaves come up spotless. In parts of Asia the lotus has stood for purity for thousands of years. In the 1970s, a botanist looking through a powerful microscope finally saw why.

How it works

The leaf is covered in microscopic bumps, each coated in wax. A raindrop sits on the tips of the bumps with air trapped underneath, so it barely touches the leaf. It rolls off and takes dirt with it.

microscopic: too small to see without a microscope.
  1. Tiny bumps cover the leaf.
  2. The bumps are coated in wax.
  3. Air gets trapped between the bumps, under the drop.
  4. Water sticks to itself more than to the leaf, so it pulls into a ball.
  5. The ball rolls off and carries dirt away.
How sure scientists are

SettledTested many times, on many kinds of plants.

It stops working when:

  • dew forms down between the bumps
  • the leaf gets scratched or rubbed Maya found this!
  • the water is soapy
